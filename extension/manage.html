About Valid Vault

Valid Vault Password Manager v0.6.0

A local, encrypted password manager. Your credentials live on your device, encrypted under keys only you can produce. No cloud, no accounts, no sync servers. Unlock with your device fingerprint or PIN, or your password. Move credentials between devices over a local QR stream or an encrypted backup file.
