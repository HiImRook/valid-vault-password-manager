Misc Credentials

Store a secret under a category, like a Wi-Fi password, license key, or security code.

Personal Info

Used by autofill on signup and profile forms. Viewing requires unlock; adding or editing any field asks for your fingerprint or device PIN, with your master password as the fallback. Social Security numbers and card info are deferred for now, held back for later, added security work.

Crypto Wallets

Store seed phrases by wallet and account. Words stay hidden until you click Show, and hide again when you switch tabs, leave the window, or the timer runs out.

Sync

Share

Show a QR for another device to scan. The vault stays encrypted under your master key, and the master key is wrapped under your passphrase and three security answers, so the receiving device must enter them before it can use the key.

Backup Files

Save an encrypted copy offline, or restore one. Files stay useless without your master key.

Import From Another App

Bring your logins over from another password manager's export file: CSV, Samsung Pass, Bitwarden JSON, 1Password 1PUX, or a Dashlane zip. Bring your bookmarks straight from this browser, or from another browser's bookmarks HTML file. Everything is encrypted into your vault as it comes in. Valid Vault never exports passwords unencrypted.

Uni-Vault

Link every browser on this computer to one encrypted vault file so they share one vault and stay in sync automatically. Export Vault creates the file; Import Vault links another browser to it.

Password Generator

Minimum length
Shortest password in the 24 range. Higher ranges start above 24.
Maximum length
Passwords land in the range for your choice: 24 gives minimum to 24, 32 gives 25 to 32, 48 gives 33 to 48, 64 gives 49 to 64. Longer passwords run into sites with hidden length limits more often. A limit a site declares always wins.

Clear Vault

This will permanently delete all stored credentials, auth methods, and settings. This cannot be undone.

Recommendation: Back up your vault to another device using Secure Sync before clearing.

About Valid Vault

Valid Vault Password Manager

Hi, I'm Rook.

I made a password manager. I found myself wanting two things I just wasn't getting: relevant features and far less betrayal.

This is my best effort at common sense features with low friction, and how about we try zero betrayal?

Valid Vault is fully local. The only person who sees, or even touches, your data is you. There is no cloud sync, by design. To me, the idea of this data, YOUR data, traveling across countries and even continents and then back to you is insane. That data never needed to leave your device. More things should be handled locally, and cloud connectivity should be approached with caution. Unfortunately, we see the opposite.

Take back your data with the Valid ecosystem. Privacy should not come at a premium. Join the Discord to connect with other privacy-minded people.
