User Guide

How Valid Vault works

Everything is encrypted on this phone under one master key. Nothing goes to a server, and there is no account. Your fingerprint, device PIN, or master password each unlock that same key.

Each section (logins, misc credentials, personal info, crypto wallets) asks you to unlock again before it shows anything.

Autofill in other apps and browsers

Turn it on in Settings, Autofill, Turn on, then choose Valid Vault. In Chrome, also turn on Settings, Passwords, Autofill using another service.

Tap a login field and choose Valid Vault from the suggestions. Confirm with your fingerprint, device PIN, or password. With one saved login for the site it fills right away; with several, pick the one you want. Only that login is handed to Android.

In browsers, Valid Vault matches the website the same way the extension does. Apps only tell Android their name, so the first time you fill in an app you pick which saved site it belongs to. Settings, Linked apps lists these links and lets you remove one.

When you sign in somewhere new, Android offers to save the login to Valid Vault. Confirm with your fingerprint and it is encrypted like any other login.

To save a page from any app as a bookmark, tap Share and choose Valid Vault.

Valid Vault has two home screen widgets. The small one is a Search privately bar with a Bookmarks button. The larger one adds Bookmarks, Logins, Wallet, and Personal buttons. Each button opens Valid Vault, asks for your fingerprint or device PIN once, and goes straight to that section.

Private browser

Valid Vault includes a Chromium browser for private browsing. Open it from Settings, from a Valid Vault widget, or by long-pressing the app icon and tapping Search. Tapping a bookmark opens it there too.

The bar along the bottom has Back, Bookmarks, Home, Share, and Forward. Both bars slide away as you scroll down a page and come back when you scroll up. Bookmarks opens Valid Vault and asks for your fingerprint or device PIN. The menu at the top right has Reload, Copy link, the tracker switch for the site, Open in another app, and Close and wipe.

Type a web address to go straight to it, or anything else to search. Startpage is the default search engine. DuckDuckGo and Brave Search are in Settings, Browser.

Known trackers and ad scripts are blocked. Tracking tags such as utm and fbclid, and share tags like YouTube's si, are removed from links, and redirect wrappers from Google, Facebook, and others are skipped so you land on the real page. Third-party cookies are off.

The number at the left of the address bar shows how many trackers were blocked on the page. If a site breaks, tap it to turn blocking off for that site. Settings, Browser lists those sites so you can turn blocking back on.

Closing the browser wipes cookies, site data, cache, and history. It runs separately from your vault and never sees your vault data.

For Tor or a VPN, run an app such as Orbot in VPN mode. The browser's traffic goes through it like any other app.

Moving to or from another device

Both devices need the same master key before vaults can merge. Do it in this order:

1. On the device that already has your vault, use Share Master Key, or Export Key to make a key file.

2. On the new device, scan that key or open the file, then enter the passphrase and three answers.

3. Then move the vault: Share Vault and scan it, or Export Vault and import the file. Vault files carry everything, including bookmarks and crypto wallets. The vault QR carries logins, misc credentials, and personal info.

Importing a vault merges it. The newest edit of each item wins, deleted items stay deleted, and the same login never shows up twice. Importing the same file again changes nothing.

Backups and USB drives

Export Vault saves an encrypted file to Downloads, or sends it with any app you choose. Copy it to a USB drive or another computer for a cold backup. It is useless without your master key.

Export Key saves your master key wrapped under your passphrase and three answers. Keep it separate from the vault file if you can.

Unlocking and auto-lock

Fingerprint or device PIN is the quickest way in. The secret stays in the phone's secure hardware. A master password works as a backup.

If you add a new fingerprint to your phone, Android resets Valid Vault's fingerprint unlock for safety. Unlock with your password, then re-enroll in Manage.

The vault locks after the idle time in Settings, or when you tap Lock.

What can and cannot be recovered

Nothing is lost while you can still unlock on any device that has your vault. A forgotten master password can be replaced from Manage while you are unlocked.

If this phone will not unlock, for example after Android resets fingerprint unlock and no password is set, tap Can't unlock? on the lock screen and use your key file or a key QR from your other device.

A lost key-file passphrase or answers cannot be recovered by anyone. Write them down and store them safely.

Scanning safely

A shared key QR is protected by your passphrase and answers. A shared vault QR is encrypted under your master key. Still, scan somewhere private.

About Valid Vault

Valid Vault Password Manager

Hi, I'm Rook.

I made a password manager. I found myself wanting two things I just wasn't getting: relevant features and far less betrayal.

This is my best effort at common sense features with low friction, and how about we try zero betrayal?

Valid Vault is fully local. The only person who sees, or even touches, your data is you. There is no cloud sync, by design. To me, the idea of this data, YOUR data, traveling across countries and even continents and then back to you is insane. That data never needed to leave your device. More things should be handled locally, and cloud connectivity should be approached with caution. Unfortunately, we see the opposite.
