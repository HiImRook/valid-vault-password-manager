Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Short version: Valid Vault collects nothing, transmits nothing, and has no servers. Your passwords are encrypted and stored only on your own device. There is no account, no analytics, and no data leaves your device unless you move it yourself.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to the Valid Vault browser extension and Android app. Both are built from the same open-source code and follow the same principle: your data stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Valid Vault has no servers and no backend. It does not create an account, does not track you, does not include analytics or telemetry, and does not send your information anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Valid Vault stores the following locally on your device, encrypted, so the app can function as a password manager:

  • The credentials you save (site, username, password), encrypted with WebCrypto
  • Your chosen unlock method (fingerprint via WebAuthn, PIN, or password)
  • Your app settings

This data lives in your browser's local storage or your device's app storage. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

Permissions the extension requests

The browser extension requests certain permissions strictly to function as a password manager. Each is used locally on your device and never used to collect or transmit data:

  • Host access — to detect login forms on the sites you visit and fill your saved credentials into them. Autofill cannot work without access to the pages where credentials are entered. No page content is collected or sent anywhere.
  • activeTab — to read the domain of the page you are on so the extension can offer the matching saved credentials.
  • Storage — to save your encrypted vault and settings locally on your device.

Syncing between your devices

Valid Vault can move your logins between your own devices using QR codes. One device displays your vault or a sync key as a QR code, and another device scans it. This happens directly between your devices, with no server and no back channel involved. The data travels only in the scan itself, so you control when and where it happens.

Third parties

Valid Vault does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect or transmit data in the first place. There are no advertisers, no analytics providers, and no external services receiving your information.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you are in full control of it. You can clear your vault from within the app's settings at any time, or remove the extension or uninstall the app to remove its stored data from your device.

Children's privacy

Valid Vault is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Because the app collects nothing, changes are expected to be rare.

Contact
