Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Scope

This Privacy Policy covers the Valid Vault browser extension (for Chrome, Edge, and Brave) and the Valid Vault Android app, which includes a private browser. Both share the same vault, encryption, and sync code, but they run in different environments, so each point below says whether it applies to the extension, the phone app, or both the extension and the phone app.

Information We Collect

Both the extension and the phone app: Valid Vault does not collect any information. There are no servers, no backend, and no user accounts. There is no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising, and nothing you store in Valid Vault is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Information Stored on Your Device

Both the extension and the phone app store the following on your device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a master key only you can unlock:

  • Website logins you save, including site, username, password, any extra form fields you chose to save with a login, when a login was last filled, and a replaced password kept for 30 days so it can be restored after a change
  • Personal information you enter for form filling, such as name, phone, address, and email addresses
  • Miscellaneous credentials you enter, such as Wi-Fi passwords or license keys
  • Crypto wallet seed phrases and notes you enter
  • Bookmarks you save, including page address and title
  • Your unlock methods, stored only as wrapped copies of the master key, never as passwords or password hashes
  • Phone app only: which Android apps a saved login is linked to, with each app's signing certificate, so a login fills only in the app it belongs to

Settings are stored on the device without encryption, since they hold no secrets: the auto-lock time, QR timeout, password generator length, the key and vault name, and day or night mode. Phone app only: the private browser also keeps its search engine choice, your new tab quick view sites, and the sites where you turned tracker blocking off.

Extension: the vault is kept in the extension's own storage in your browser profile, along with an encrypted backup copy that restores the vault if the browser clears site data. When you submit a login form, the captured login is held in the browser's session memory for up to 45 seconds so the save prompt can survive a sign-in redirect. It is cleared when you answer the prompt, when the vault locks, or when the browser closes.

Phone app: the vault is kept in the app's private storage. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the app's encrypted vault in your phone's own backup, where it stays encrypted under your master key. Fingerprint keys stay in the phone's hardware keystore and are never backed up. The private browser's data and settings are excluded from backups.

Permissions

Extension: each permission is used on your device only, to operate as a password manager:

  • Host access (all websites): to detect login, signup, and personal info forms on websites you visit, show the Valid Vault tag on matched fields, and fill your saved details when you click a tag. Field attributes are read to identify fields. When you submit a login form, the username, the password, and any other filled-in fields on that form, such as a PIN or account number box, are read so Valid Vault can offer to save them with the login and fill them the next time. They are saved only if you choose Save, they are stored encrypted in your vault, and they never leave your device. No other page content is read, stored, or transmitted.
  • activeTab: to read the address and title of the current tab when you open the popup, so the page can be saved to or removed from your bookmarks.
  • Storage: to store the encrypted vault and settings in the extension's own storage.
  • Unlimited storage: so the browser keeps the encrypted vault and its backup copy instead of clearing them when space runs low.
  • Alarms: to lock the vault after the idle time you choose.
  • Side panel: to show your encrypted bookmarks in the browser side panel.
  • Bookmarks (optional): asked for only when you click Import This Browser's Bookmarks. The browser's bookmarks are read once, encrypted into your vault, and the permission is given back right after.

The extension's own pages are blocked from making network requests by the extension's content security policy, so the popup, Manage, the unlock window, and the bookmarks panel cannot connect to any server.

Phone app:

  • Camera: used only while the QR scanner is open, to read a QR code shown by your other device. Frames are read on the phone and are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Internet: used only by the private browser, to load the pages you open and to reach Orbot on your phone in Tor mode. The vault screens contain no code that makes network requests.
  • Fingerprint and device PIN: Android shows its own fingerprint or PIN prompt and tells Valid Vault only whether it succeeded. Valid Vault never sees your fingerprint or PIN.
  • Autofill service (optional): if you set Valid Vault as the phone's autofill service, Android tells it which fields a form in another app has, never what is typed in them. A login is handed to Android only after your fingerprint, device PIN, or password, and only the one you pick. When Android offers to save a login, the typed login is held in memory until you confirm or dismiss it.
  • Storage, Android 9 and older only: to save exported files, downloads, and start page backgrounds to the Downloads folder. Newer Android versions save there without this permission.
  • Seeing whether Orbot is installed: so Tor mode can check that Orbot is there before it turns on.

Phone app: when you share a link to Valid Vault from another app, it is saved as an encrypted bookmark. Home screen widgets and shortcuts only open the app and ask for your fingerprint or device PIN; they show no vault contents.

Private Browser

Phone app only: the private browser loads pages directly from the websites you visit, like any browser, so those sites receive your requests and see what any site sees, including your IP address unless you use Tor mode or a VPN. Your browsing is never sent to the developer.

  • What you type in the address bar that is not a web address is sent as a search to the search engine you choose in Settings: Startpage by default, or DuckDuckGo or Brave Search.
  • Tracker blocking runs on the phone against lists bundled with the app. Nothing is looked up online.
  • Every page receives a Global Privacy Control signal asking it not to sell or share your data.
  • Cookies, site data, cache, and history are deleted when you close the browser. The browser runs in its own process with its own storage, apart from the vault.
  • In Tor mode, every page goes through Orbot, a separate app you install from the Tor Project, and nothing loads without it.
  • Downloads are saved to the Downloads folder only after you confirm, and a start page background is saved only when you tap Save it to Downloads. A saved background holds only the picture.
  • Valid Vault autofill in the browser fills a login only into the page it was requested for, after your fingerprint, device PIN, or password. In Tor mode, autofill only runs from the menu.

Activity and Auto-Lock

Extension: to keep the vault open while you are using your browser, the extension notes that real mouse, scroll, click, or key activity happened and resets the auto-lock timer. It does not record what you typed, what you clicked, or where. Only the time of the last activity is kept, in memory, and it is cleared when the browser closes.

Phone app: the auto-lock timer resets when you use the app. Only the time of the last activity is kept, in memory.

Device-to-Device Sync and Files

Both the extension and the phone app: the vault moves between your own devices by QR code or by an exported file. One device shows the encrypted vault as a QR code and the other scans it, or you save the encrypted file and open it on the other device yourself. The vault stays encrypted throughout, and the master key only leaves a device wrapped under a passphrase and three security questions you choose. No server or outside service is involved.

Both the extension and the phone app: import files from other password managers are read on the device and encrypted straight into the vault. Valid Vault never writes passwords to a file unencrypted.

Extension only: Uni-Vault links the browsers on one computer to an encrypted vault file you pick, which Valid Vault reads and writes through the browser's file access. The file stays encrypted and stays where you put it.

Phone app only: an exported file can be saved to Downloads or sent with Android's share menu. If you send it to another app, that app handles the file from then on. The file is encrypted and useless without your master key.

Third Parties

Both the extension and the phone app: Valid Vault does not share information with third parties and contains no advertising, analytics providers, or outside services that receive your information.

Phone app only: websites you visit in the private browser, the search engine you choose, and Orbot in Tor mode receive the requests needed to load what you asked for, as with any browser. Valid Vault does not send them anything from your vault, except a login or personal detail you choose to fill into that site.

Data Retention and Deletion

Both the extension and the phone app: all data stays on your device under your control. Clear Vault in Settings deletes the vault and your unlock methods.

Extension: Clear Vault also deletes the encrypted backup copy, and removing the extension deletes everything it stored.

Phone app: uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone. A copy in your Android backup, if you use one, is kept and deleted by Android's backup settings. Files you exported stay wherever you saved or sent them until you delete them.

Children's Privacy

Both the extension and the phone app: Valid Vault is a general-purpose application and is not directed at children. It does not collect personal information from any user; anything entered stays encrypted on the user's own device.
