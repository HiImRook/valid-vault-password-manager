VALID VAULT 

A local, encrypted password manager. No cloud, no accounts. Your credentials never leave your device.

Open source and free. Browser extension and Android app.

Get it on GitHub Privacy Policy
the idea

No server means nothing to breach

Most password managers store your vault on their servers. That makes their servers a target, and your passwords are only as safe as someone else's infrastructure.

Valid Vault keeps everything on your own device, encrypted. There is no account to create and no cloud to trust, because your vault never leaves your hands. When you want your logins on another device, you move them yourself by scanning a QR code.

what it does

Features

Local encryption
Every credential is encrypted on your device with WebCrypto and stored locally. Nothing is transmitted to any server.
Fingerprint unlock
Unlock with your device's fingerprint sensor through WebAuthn, or use a PIN or password.
Autofill
Detects login forms on the sites you visit and fills your saved credentials.
QR sync between your devices
Move your logins between your own devices by showing a QR code on one and scanning it with another. No back channel, no server.
how sync works

You move it, nothing else does

  1. Show. One device displays your vault, or a sync key, as a scrolling QR code.
  2. Scan. The other device scans it with its camera.
  3. Merge. The second device writes its own copy and merges. Done.
The key and vault travel in the scan itself. Do it somewhere private, the same trust you rely on when typing a password. There is no server and no back channel involved.
